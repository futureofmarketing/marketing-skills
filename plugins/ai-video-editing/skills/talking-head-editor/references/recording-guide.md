# Recording Guide: One Good Take

The edit starts while you record. These habits make one take enough.

- **Horizontal, 1080p or higher,** so there's room for visuals next to your face. The vertical Reel is made later.
- **Eye-level camera, light from the front,** and some space around your head for reframing.
- **One idea per sentence.** Each sentence becomes one visual.
- **A short pause between sentences.** The editor cuts in the pauses.
- **Stumbled? Pause and repeat the whole sentence.** The editor keeps the last complete version.
- **Know your script, don't read it.** Bullet points beat a teleprompter.
- **45 to 90 seconds** is the sweet spot for a first video.

Before you hand it over, put into the folder any logo, screenshot or product image you want to see in the video, and a music track you have the rights to use. The more precise your input, the closer the result gets to what you have in mind.
